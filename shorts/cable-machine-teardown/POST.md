# Post: Why 50 lb on the cable machine feels lighter

**Caption**
Why does 50 lb on the cable machine feel so light? 🏋️ Many cable stations are 2:1: the stack hangs from a movable pulley, two strands share the load, so your hand feels about half, and the handle travels twice as far. Same work, force traded for distance. Add a few percent of friction per pulley and 50 on the pin feels like ~30 pulling, ~20 letting go. Some machines are 4:1: a quarter of the force, four times the travel.

Check your machine's ratio sticker before comparing numbers between gyms.

Sources: Wikipedia (Block and tackle); Fringe Sport, Rep Fitness, MuscleSquad and Skelcore cable-ratio guides; US patent 7601105; Lexco/Loos & Co. wire-rope stretch formula. Friction and stretch figures are worked estimates (see FACTS). Schematic model, not a specific machine.

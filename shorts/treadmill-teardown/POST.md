# Post: How a Treadmill Knows Your Speed

**Caption**
How does a treadmill know how fast you're running? 🏃 It counts. A tiny magnet on the front roller passes a sensor once per turn, and speed = roller rpm × circumference: a 2.5 in roller at 700 rpm is about 5.2 mph. Every foot strike brakes the belt 4–9 %, and the controller pushes more current to catch it. And yes, the 1% incline thing is real: Jones & Doust (1996) found 1% grade matches the energy cost of running outside.

Sources: Jones & Doust, J. Sports Sci. 1996; Radstake, McMaster University 2000; Savelberg et al., Gait & Posture 1998; Precor and ProForm service manuals; US Patent 5,368,532. Schematic model, not to scale, no brand.

# Post: The EUV Machine Behind Every AI Chip

**Caption**
What if one machine controlled the future of AI? 🤖 Only one company on Earth builds EUV lithography machines. Tin droplets hit by a CO₂ laser 50,000×/s → plasma ~40× hotter than the Sun's surface → 13.5 nm light, bounced off mirrors so smooth that scaled to Germany the tallest bump is ~1 mm → transistor gates ~20 nm wide. ~$150M each (newest High-NA ~$380–400M), shipped in 40 containers on 3 planes. None has ever been shipped to China.

Sources: ASML; TRUMPF; IEEE Spectrum; Wikipedia (EUV lithography); Live Science; Works in Progress; Tom's Hardware; US BIS (Oct 2022 rule). Schematic model, not to scale, no logos.

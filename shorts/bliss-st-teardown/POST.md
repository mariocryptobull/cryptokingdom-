# Post: How 46 St–Bliss St Was Built

**Caption**
How was this train station built? 🚇 The 7 train's 46 St–Bliss St station in Sunnyside, Queens stands on a concrete viaduct from 1917. The city wanted Queens Boulevard beautiful, so engineers skipped the usual steel el and built reinforced concrete arches. Here's the whole life of the station, from the 1913 Dual Contracts to the rebuild happening right now.

Sources: Engineering News (Nov 11, 1915); nycsubway.org; MTA; Forgotten New York; Wikipedia. Schematic model, not to scale.

# Post: What happens when computers start using human brains?

**Caption**
What happens when computers start using human brains? 🧠💻 This is a biocomputer: real, living human neurons grown from stem cells on a chip with 59 electrodes. The box is their body: pumps, waste filters, gas mixing and heat keep them alive for up to six months. In 2022, about 800,000 neurons in a dish (DishBrain) learned Pong. Its successor, Cortical Labs' CL1, sells for $35,000, and Swiss startup FinalSpark rents out 16 living brain organoids online. Why? Your brain runs on ~20 W; AI data centers draw hundreds of megawatts. FinalSpark's "a million times less energy" is a company claim, not proven.

Sources: Kagan et al., Neuron (2022); NPR; Tom's Hardware; New Atlas; Business Wire (FinalSpark); Data Center Frontier. Schematic model, not to scale, no brand hardware shown.

# Post: How Air Flows Through a Vent

**Caption**
Why does the air from a vent hug the ceiling? 🌬️ Follow it from the blower: about half an inch of water of pressure pushes air down the main duct at ~900 ft/min. It speeds up where the duct narrows, slows where it widens, and pays for every elbow in friction. Branches run ~600 ft/min. At the diffuser, angled vanes throw it sideways, and the low-pressure gap between the fast jet and the ceiling pulls it flat against the surface: the Coandă effect. Turbulent eddies slow it to 50 ft/min by the end of its throw, so you feel a gentle draft under 40 ft/min.

Colour = air speed (blue slow, orange fast). Schematic model, not to scale.
Sources: ACCA Manual D velocity guidance; ASHRAE Standard 55; Krueger/Conklin diffuser data; LBNL/AHRI static-pressure research.

# Post: How a Pedal Moves a Car

**Caption**
How does pressing a pedal make a car move? 🚗 Gas pedal: two sensors tell the engine computer how far you pressed, a motor opens the throttle, air + fuel (about 14.7 : 1 by weight) burn, pistons spin the crankshaft, and the gearbox and differential multiply that twist ~14× in first gear before four hand-sized patches of rubber push the road back. Brake pedal: a 4 : 1 lever and a vacuum booster turn a 100 lb push into ~800 lb, a 1-inch master cylinder makes ~1,000 psi, and Pascal's law turns that into ~5,000 lb of clamping force per caliper.

Worked example for a hard stop with typical values; real cars vary. Sources: Wilwood; Brake & Front End / Underhood Service "Brake Math"; Wikipedia (Air–fuel ratio); Continental Tire; Haltech. Schematic model, not to scale.

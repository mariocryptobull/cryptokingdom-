# Tesla Optimus: facts and sources

Direct page fetches are blocked on this network; facts come from search-result text of the pages below (checked Nov 14, 2026).
The model in the video is schematic: placement of the 28 actuators, the shells and the hand are illustrative, not Tesla's layout.

| Claim in script / on screen | Source(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 28 structural actuators in the body | Tesla AI Day 2022, via humanoid.guide ("28 structural actuators built from six designs"); chipsetc.com; teslaoracle.com | First-gen prototype figure. |
| Six actuator designs: three rotary, three linear | humanoid.guide "Linear vs Rotary Actuators in Humanoid Joints" (rotary 20 / 110 / 180 N·m; linear 500 / 3,900 / 8,000 N); humanoid.guide "How Many Actuator Types…" | Robotics 24/7 reports an optimization that consolidated to seven types; six is the figure Tesla presented and most sources repeat. |
| Rotary twists joints; linear pushes "like muscles" | Same AI Day sources (rotary units with harmonic reducers; linear units with roller screws in knees/hips/ankles) | Plain-language description. |
| Linear actuators use planetary roller screws | humanoid.guide "Planetary Roller Screws in Humanoid Robots"; kggfa.com "The Tesla robot: the planetary roller screw"; 36Kr via optimusk.blog (≈14 inverted roller screws in hips, knees, ankles) | |
| One lifted a ~half-tonne piano | humanoid.guide: one linear unit was shown lifting a roughly 500 kg concert grand piano (AI Day 2022) | Spoken "half-tonne"; on screen "~500 kg". |
| Hands: 22 degrees of freedom; double the first version (11) | Wikipedia "Optimus (robot)" (Nov 2024: Tesla showed a new hand with 22 DoF); IEEE Spectrum (Gen 1: 6 actuators, 11 DoF per hand); TeslaMagz / Teslarati (V3 patent WO2026080687, tendon-driven, actuators in forearm) | "Latest public hand (2024)". | 2.3 kWh battery pack in the torso (52 V) | Tesla AI Day 2022 spec via chipsetc.com / advancedbatteriesresearch.com (IDTechEx) / notateslaapp.com | AI Day (Gen 1) figure; Tesla has not restated it for later versions. |

| Learns from human demos: suits, cameras, real tasks | electrek / eWeek: workers in motion-capture suits; later helmet-and-backpack camera rigs; Tesla (May 2025) said tasks were learned from videos of humans (teslanorth.com, benzinga.com) | |

# Post: Tesla Optimus Teardown

**Caption**
How will robots rise to power? 🤖 They won't. Tesla's Optimus was built to work. Inside: 28 actuators in six designs (three rotary, three linear), planetary roller screws, 22-DoF hands, a 2.3 kWh torso battery, Autopilot cameras and a self-driving computer. It learns from human demos and simulation. Tesla's goals: factories first, a line built for 20,000 a week, and a $20–30k price. Goals, not done yet.

Sources: Tesla AI Day 2022; Tesla Q3 2026 earnings call; IEEE Spectrum; Wikipedia (Optimus); humanoid.guide; Electrek. Schematic model, not Tesla's design.

# Post: How AI Data Centers Use Water

**Caption**
Is AI drinking the planet dry? 💧 AI chips run so hot they need liquid cooling: cold plates on every GPU, a coolant loop, a chiller, and rooftop cooling towers that evaporate water to dump the heat. Operators report 0.15 L/kWh (AWS, 2024) and 0.27 L/kWh (Microsoft, 2025) on site, but the power plants behind them use far more: about 12× more for US data centers in 2023 (LBNL). Google puts one median Gemini text prompt at 0.26 mL, about five drops. The largest sites can use up to 5 million gallons a day (EESI). Fixes: closed loops, dry coolers, cooler climates.

Sources: NVIDIA; AWS; Microsoft; Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (via The Conversation); Google (Aug 2025); EESI. Schematic model, not to scale.

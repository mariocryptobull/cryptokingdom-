# Post: How the Holland Tunnel Breathes

**Caption**
Thousands of cars in a tube under the Hudson. Why doesn't the exhaust kill everyone? 🌬️ The 1927 Holland Tunnel splits each tube into three layers: fans blow fresh air into a duct under the roadway, it rises through slots at the curb, and dirty air is pulled up through ceiling vents into an exhaust duct and out. Four ventilation buildings (two on land, two in the river) hold 84 fans, 42 in and 42 out. At full speed they can replace all the air every 90 seconds. Designed by Ole Singstad after chief engineer Clifford Holland died in 1924. The world's first mechanically ventilated tunnel and a National Historic Landmark.

# Post: How the Brooklyn Bridge Was Built

**Caption**
How was the Brooklyn Bridge built? 🌉 Timber caissons sunk 44 and 78 feet under the East River, granite towers, 60,000-ton anchorages, four steel cables spun wire by wire, and a deck hung from suspenders and diagonal stays. It opened May 24, 1883, as the longest suspension bridge on Earth, and it took the whole Roebling family to finish it.

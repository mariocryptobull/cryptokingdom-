# Post: How Neuralink Works

**Caption**
This is Elon Musk's Neuralink 🧠 A coin-sized implant with a wirelessly charged battery, a custom chip and 64 ultra-thin threads carrying 1,024 electrodes, placed by the R1 surgical robot. Neurons fire, electrodes catch the spikes, the chip sends them over Bluetooth, and software turns intent into cursor movement. The first participant (2024) played chess by thought; Neuralink reported 21 participants by Jan 2026. Phones, computers, even cars by thought? That's the goal, not yet demonstrated.

Sources: Neuralink; Reuters; Bloomberg; Barrow Neurological Institute; Popular Science. Schematic model, not to scale, not affiliated with Neuralink.

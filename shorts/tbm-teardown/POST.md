# Post: How a Tunnel Boring Machine Works

**Caption**
How do you dig a subway tunnel without tearing up the city? 🚇 You send in a tunnel boring machine: a spinning cutterhead up front, a moving factory behind it, building the tunnel lining ring by ring. Cut, muck, ring, push, around the clock. NYC's Second Avenue Subway machine "Adi" was 450 ft long and averaged about 60 ft a day under Manhattan.

Sources: MTA; Wikipedia (Tunnel boring machine); Trenchlesspedia; Tunnelling Journal. Schematic model, not to scale.

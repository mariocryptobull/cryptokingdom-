# Post: How a Subway Train Rides Its Rails

**Caption**
How does a NYC subway train actually ride its rails? 🚇 We took the track apart: ~115 lb/yd steel rails 4 ft 8½ in apart, tie plates on wood ties or concrete blocks, ballast or a concrete bed, and a 625-volt third rail under a cover board. A contact shoe slides on it to power the motors. The wheels are slightly cone-shaped, so in a curve the outer wheel rolls farther and the train steers itself, all on contact patches about the size of a dime.

⚠️ That's also why subway surfing kills: tunnel ceilings sit inches above the roof, curves and hard braking throw you, and 625 V runs below. NYC lost 6 people to it in 2024 and at least 5 in 2025 (NYPD/MTA). Ride inside. Stay alive. Subway surfing kills. Don't.

Sources: MTA, NYPD (via AP, PIX11), nycsubway.org, Wikipedia (Third rail, Wheelset), WMATA. Schematic model, not to scale; full list in FACTS.md.

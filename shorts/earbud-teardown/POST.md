# Post: What's inside an AirPod?

**Caption**
What's inside an AirPod? 🎧 We scaled an AirPods-style earbud up 100× and took it apart: a lithium-ion button cell (49.7 mAh in AirPods Pro 2), the main chip, an antenna, a speaker driver (magnet, voice coil, diaphragm), beamforming mics, a pressure vent, a force sensor and a skin sensor. Noise cancellation = the outer mic hears the noise, the driver plays the flipped wave, and they cancel. The catch: it's glued shut. iFixit scored them 0/10 for repairability.

Reference: Apple AirPods Pro / AirPods Pro 2. Generic schematic model, no logos, not affiliated with Apple.
Sources: iFixit teardowns; MacRumors / GSMArena (3C battery filings); Apple product and support pages. Full list in FACTS.md.

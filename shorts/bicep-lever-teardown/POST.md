# Post: Your Bicep Is a Lever (and It's Losing)

**Caption**
Your biceps is a third-class lever, and it's built to lose on force. 💪⚙️ The tendon anchors ~4 cm from the elbow, the dumbbell sits ~35 cm out. Holding 10 kg (98 N) level = 34 N·m of torque, so the biceps has to pull ~860 N: nearly 9× the weight. The payoff is speed: 1 cm of muscle shortening swings your hand ~9 cm. And the load peaks at 90°, the sticking point of every curl.

Sources: OpenOregon Body Physics ("Body Levers"); Murray, Delp & Buchanan (J Biomech 1995, 2002); US anthropometric survey data. Schematic model; forearm weight ignored; numbers are typical, not universal.

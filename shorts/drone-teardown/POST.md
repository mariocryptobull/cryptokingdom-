# Post: What's inside a military recon drone?

**Caption**
What's actually inside a military recon drone? 🛩️ A hand-launched plane under 2 kg: electric motor and prop, a battery good for about an hour, a flight controller (gyros, accelerometers, GPS), an autopilot that flies waypoints, a radio datalink, and a stabilized day/thermal camera turret.

And Palantir? It doesn't build the drone. It builds software: the Pentagon's Maven Smart System, largely built on Palantir's platform, fuses drone video with other feeds, flags objects with computer vision, and maps them for human analysts.

# Post: Why AI Is Restarting Nuclear Plants

**Caption**
Will AI bring about nuclear disaster? ☢️ Microsoft signed a 20-year deal to restart Unit 1 at Three Mile Island, the site of America's worst nuclear accident (1979, Unit 2). Inside: uranium fuel rods make heat, control rods tame it, a pressurized loop boils a second loop, steam spins a turbine, the generator makes 835 MW, 24/7 and carbon-free. Why now? Data centers used 4.4% of US power in 2023 and could hit 6.7–12% by 2028. Google and Amazon are planning small modular reactors (first by 2030 / 5 GW by 2039: plans). The catch: $1.6B to restart, years to build new ones, and no permanent home for the waste. Restart target 2027, pending NRC approval.

Sources: NRC; Constellation; World Nuclear News; LBNL/US DOE (2024); Google/Kairos and Amazon/X-energy announcements; CRS; GAO. Schematic model, not to scale.

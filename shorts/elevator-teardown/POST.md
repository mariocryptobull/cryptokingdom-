# Post: How an Elevator Works

**Caption**
How does a steel box carry you up a hundred floors? 🛗 A traction elevator hangs the car on steel ropes over a grooved sheave, with a counterweight on the other side, so the motor only moves the difference. If the car ever overspeeds, a governor trips and safety brakes clamp the guide rails. That idea goes back to 1854, when Elisha Otis had the rope cut under his own platform in New York.

Sources: Britannica (Elisha Otis); ASME A17.1 via California Title 8 elevator orders; Elevator World; US patent 4,754,850. Schematic model, not to scale.

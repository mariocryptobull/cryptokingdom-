# Post: How a Jet Engine Works

**Caption**
How does a jet engine push an airliner through the sky? ✈️ We took a turbofan apart: the giant fan sends most of the air around the core (that bypass air makes most of the thrust), the compressor squeezes the rest 30×+, the combustor burns fuel nonstop, and the turbine spins the shafts that drive it all. Air goes back, engine goes forward. Suck, squeeze, bang, blow. The Boeing 737's CFM56-7B: 61-in fan, ~5:1 bypass, up to 27,300 lbf of thrust.

Sources: Aircraft Commerce CFM56-7B guide; Purdue AAE; Wikipedia (Turbofan); planefyi. Schematic model, not to scale.

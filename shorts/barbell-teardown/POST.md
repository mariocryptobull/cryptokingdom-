# Post: What's Inside an Olympic Barbell?

**Caption**
What's actually inside a 20 kg Olympic barbell? 🏋️ A 28 mm alloy-steel shaft rated around 190,000 PSI, a diamond knurl, grip marks 910 mm apart, and sleeves that spin on bronze bushings or needle bearings so the plates stay put while the bar turns over in a snatch or clean. Load it heavy and it flexes like a spring: that's whip.

Sources: IWF equipment rules; Eleiko & Strength Shop on bar rotation; Garage Gym Reviews / American Barbell spec sheets (tensile ratings); Acoustical Society of America (barbell whip research). Schematic model, no brand. Full list in FACTS.md.

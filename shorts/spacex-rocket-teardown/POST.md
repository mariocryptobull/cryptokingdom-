# Post: Starship, Taken Apart

**Caption**
This is Starship, the biggest rocket ever flown: about 400 ft tall. 🚀 Super Heavy's 33 Raptor engines push out over 18 million lbf, burning liquid oxygen and liquid methane from stainless-steel tanks. Each Raptor runs full-flow staged combustion. The booster flies home on grid fins and the tower catches it (first done Oct 13, 2024). On Sep 28, 2026, Flight 14 reached orbit and released 26 Starlink satellites. Still planned, per SpaceX: orbital refueling, a Moon lander for NASA, and Mars.

Sources: SpaceX; NASA OIG (IG-26-004); NPR; SpacePolicyOnline; Space.com; Wikipedia (SpaceX Starship, Super Heavy, Raptor, Starship HLS). Schematic model, not to scale, no logos.
